How the war works

  • Each of the 9 points pays +4 supply/s on top of your base +14/s.
  • After the first minute, whoever holds fewer points bleeds tickets every second (0.7 per point behind, up to 4). Losing units costs tickets too. Hit 0 and you lose.
  • Destroying the enemy HQ wins outright. It is guarded by two bunker turrets.
  • Infantry in forests take less damage and stay hidden until enemies get close. Scouts and snipers also hide while standing still. Radar trucks see through it.
  • Tanks take 1.5× damage from behind. Flank them.
  • Machine guns and explosions pin infantry: pinned troops crawl and miss.
  • Artillery, barrages and missiles need someone to see the target.
  • Units heal slowly next to your HQ, faster near medics and supply trucks.
  • Kills promote units to veteran: more damage and accuracy.
