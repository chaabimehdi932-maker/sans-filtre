How the war works

  • Two AI generals fight over nine towns. You are one soldier in the blue army.
  • A town belongs to whoever has a town hall in it. Wreck the enemy's hall (tanks, rockets, satchels and artillery do it fast; soldiers standing in an undefended town wreck it slowly), then hammer up your own.
  • Hold 7 of the 9 towns for 90 seconds, or destroy the enemy HQ, to win.
  • Supply chain: scrap fields → scrap → any base turns it into building materials (bmats) → only the HQ turns bmats into supplies (ammo, grenades, bandages). Cargo trucks carry it all to the towns.
  • The generals pay for infantry with supplies and for vehicles with bmats. New infantry walks out of supplied towns near the front.
  • Everybody's ammo runs out. Soldiers rearm near a base or a cargo truck that has supplies; a town with none can't rearm or respawn anyone.
  • Trenches and sandbags make infantry much harder to hit. Barbed wire slows enemy infantry to a crawl; tanks crush it. Tank traps stop vehicles. MG bunkers shoot on their own. Watchtowers see far. Forward bases hold supplies and let you respawn.
  • Wounds bleed until you bandage them (H). A base's medics patch you up.
  • Tanks take 1.5× damage from behind. Machine guns and explosions pin infantry.
  • Artillery, barrages and V-2s need someone to see the target; your binoculars count.
