How the war works

  • Both armies are run by AI generals. You lead one squad of 3 to 8 units; the allied general runs everyone else.
  • Your squad moves at the pace of its slowest member, and every member with a gun that reaches fires when you left-click a spot.
  • Command sends you orders that fit your squad. Finish them for bonus points.
  • Lost soldiers come back while your squad rests out of combat near the HQ, a point you hold or a supply truck. Lost vehicles are replaced at the HQ.
  • When your squad is wiped out, pick a new one: infantry squads deploy at the HQ or on a point you hold, vehicle squads at the HQ.
  • After the first minute, whoever holds fewer points bleeds tickets every second (0.7 per point behind, up to 4). Losing units costs tickets too. Hit 0 and you lose.
  • Destroying the enemy HQ wins outright. It is guarded by two bunker turrets.
  • Infantry in forests take less damage and stay hidden until enemies get close. Scouts and snipers also hide while standing still. Radar trucks see through it.
  • Tanks take 1.5× damage from behind. Flank them.
  • Machine guns and explosions pin infantry: pinned troops crawl and miss.
  • Artillery, barrages and missiles need someone to see the target.
  • Units heal slowly next to the HQ, faster near medics and supply trucks.
  • Kills promote units to veteran: more damage and accuracy.
